VLSM Planner

Give it one parent network and the hosts each department needs. It hands back right-sized subnets, largest first, each aligned to its block boundary.

Rule used: a subnet needs hosts + 2 addresses (network and broadcast), rounded up to the next power of two, with a minimum block of 4 (/30). Sample project by Ejaz Ahemad, built with AI assistance.
